Whispers of My World

When I’m not coding spells into machines, I wander through worlds that feed the soul.

⚽ Beneath the Open Sky

Whether it’s a grassy pitch or a quiet alley, soccer brings me to life. It’s not just a game — it’s motion, rhythm, and instinct blending like wind through tall summer grass. I love the feeling of a perfect pass, like the wind carrying a leaf exactly where it belongs.

🌌 Eyes Toward the Stars

I often find myself looking up — chasing constellations and dreaming with planets. Stargazing gives me peace, like the still moments in a Ghibli film where time holds its breath and the universe feels just a bit closer.

🎨 Paints & Pastels

With brush in hand, I drift into a different realm. Painting is how I bring dreamscapes to life — sometimes soft and ethereal, other times bold and fantastical. I especially love watercolors and anything that captures fleeting light, like a spark of magic in the forest.

🎸 Strings & Stories

My guitar is my companion through quiet evenings. I play soft fingerstyle melodies, inspired by the warmth of Joe Hisaishi’s music — simple, serene, and full of heart. Sometimes I hum along, like a soot sprite might if it had a song.

📚 Pages and Portals

Books are my portals to new worlds. I read everything from fantasy epics to quiet slice-of-life stories. Each page is like a path through a sun-dappled forest — a place to wander, wonder, and find something new in myself.

These hobbies aren't just pastimes — they are how I connect with wonder, with stillness, with stories. Just like a Ghibli spirit, I move where the wind takes me.
